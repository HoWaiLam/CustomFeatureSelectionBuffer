DESCRIPTION

Photogrammetric models deliver realistic visuals for 3D web maps, but other supplementary data layers (such as 2D polygons) are required for functionalities such as feature selection and information retrieval. Large Discrepancies between the precieved boundary of a feature in the photogrammetric model and its counterpart in underlying data layer would adversely affect the accuracy of the selected feature and the information retrieved.

Here is an example of a photogrammetric model of several city blocks and the 2D building polygon layer used for building selection and retrieving basic building information. The exclusion of covered balconies and bay windows from the building polygon layer creates discrepancies between the polygons and the perceived building boundaries in the photogrammetric model. As a result, users may be unable to retrieve information when clicking or hovering over what appears to be part of a building.

We can reduce such discrepancies by computing a custom selection buffer for each building feature in the polygon layer. The selection buffer uses a default distance of 2.5 meters and can be adjusted individually based on the size of covered balconies and bay windows. It also accounts for distances to neighbouring buildings to prevent overlaps.
